What this page will cover

Why Fired is built around a simple rule: your case is yours. This policy will explain, in plain language, exactly what information we collect, why we collect it, and who can see it.

What we will never do

  • We will never publicly display the name of your employer.
  • We will never link your donation history to your case submission.
  • We will never sell your data to anyone.

Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

As an Indian data protection law, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act gives you rights over your own information; including the right to know what we hold and the right to ask us to delete it.
