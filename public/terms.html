What Why Fired is

Why Fired is an information and documentation tool; it helps you understand whether your termination followed a fair process and gives you a private place to record your case. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation.

Not legal advice

Nothing on this site is a legal verdict or a guarantee of any outcome. The guidance we give is general in nature; for advice specific to your situation, you should speak with a lawyer or your state labour commissioner.

Your account and your case

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you submit. We will never make your employer's name public, and verification of a case never blocks you from receiving guidance; it only affects whether the case counts toward aggregate, anonymised pattern data.
